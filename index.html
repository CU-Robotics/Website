Our robots

Three robots, designed and built by students, make up our lineup for ARC 3v3 matches.

  • Hero

    A larger, heavy-hitting robot that fires golf-ball-sized 42 mm projectiles for extra damage.

  • Sentry

    An Infantry-class robot that drives itself. It navigates the field and engages opponents with no driver.

  • Infantry / Standard

    The fast, versatile core of the lineup. It fires 17 mm projectiles.
